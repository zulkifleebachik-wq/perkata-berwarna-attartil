Kitab dan wahyuScripture and revelation

Keluarga makna · 3 kalimah al-QuranMeaning family · 3 Qur’anic words

Dalam bahasa Melayu, «kitab» dan «wahyu» dipakai untuk firman Allah yang disampaikan kepada para nabi. Al-Quran memakai tiga akar kata untuk makna ini. Al-Raghib al-Asfahani berkata asal kitabah (penulisan) ialah menyusun huruf dengan tulisan, dan kerana itu firman Allah dinamakan kitab walaupun belum ditulis. Beliau berkata asal wahy (wahyu) ialah isyarat yang cepat. Beliau juga berkata nuzul (turun) pada asalnya ialah turun dari tempat yang tinggi, dan beliau membezakan inzal (menurunkan) dengan tanzil (menurunkan beransur-ansur).In Malay, «kitab» and «wahyu» are used for God's word conveyed to the prophets. The Qur'an uses three roots for this sense. Al-Raghib al-Isfahani says kitaba (writing) basically means joining letters in script, and that is why God's word is called kitab (scripture) even when not written. He says wahy (revelation) basically means a swift indication. He also says nuzul (descending) basically means coming down from a height, and he distinguishes inzal (sending down) from tanzil (sending down in stages).

Kiraan separa. Akar ini muncul 78 kali. Dikira wahy (wahyu) daripada Allah, 73 kali, dengan semua jenis yang disebut oleh al-Raghib: kepada para nabi; ilham (bisikan ke dalam hati), seperti kepada ibu Nabi Musa (Taha 20:38 dua kali, al-Qasas 28:7); taskhir (dipaksa, ditundukkan), seperti kepada lebah (an-Nahl 16:68); wahyu kepada Hawariyyin (para pengikut setia Nabi Isa) melalui Nabi 'Isa (al-Ma'idah 5:111); kepada malaikat (al-Anfal 8:12); dan dakwaan palsu menerima wahyu (al-An'am 6:93, dua kali). Tidak dikira 5 kali. Dua daripadanya ialah Fussilat 41:12 (kepada langit) dan az-Zalzalah 99:5 (kepada bumi); kedua-duanya tidak dikira kerana al-Raghib tidak memetiknya (Rumusan AtTartil). Tiga lagi ialah Maryam 19:11, iaitu Nabi Zakaria memberi isyarat kepada kaumnya (al-Raghib menukil tiga pendapat: dia memberi isyarat samar, dia menunjuk, atau dia menulis); serta al-An'am 6:112 dan 6:121, iaitu bisikan syaitan, yang al-Raghib kaitkan dengan waswas (bisikan jahat). Akar ini tidak dipakai oleh keluarga lain.Partial count. The root occurs 78 times. Wahy (revelation) from God is counted, 73 times, with all the kinds al-Raghib names: to prophets; ilham (inspiration into the heart), as to Moses' mother (Ta Ha 20:38 twice, al-Qasas 28:7); taskhir (subjugation), as to the bee (al-Nahl 16:68); revelation to Hawariyyin (Jesus' disciples) through Jesus (al-Ma'ida 5:111); to angels (al-Anfal 8:12); and a false claim to receive revelation (al-An'am 6:93, twice). Not counted, 5 times. Two are Fussilat 41:12 (to the heavens) and al-Zalzala 99:5 (to the earth); neither is counted because al-Raghib does not cite them (AtTartil's summary). The other three are Maryam 19:11, Zechariah signalling to his people (al-Raghib reports three views: he hinted, he pointed, or he wrote); and al-An'am 6:112 and 6:121, the devils' whispering, which al-Raghib links with waswas (evil whispering). This root is not used by any other family.

Al-Raghib says inzal (sending down) is also used of God's favours and punishments; so not counted are: rain and water, provision, the food from heaven for the Children of Israel (2:57), Jesus' table (5:112–115), clothing (7:26), iron (57:25), cattle (39:6), tranquillity and hosts (9:26), angels descending (e.g. 6:8, 97:4), punishment (e.g. 2:59, 29:34), hospitality for guests (e.g. 32:19), the moon's stations (10:5, 36:39) and the host who gives lodging (12:59, 23:29). Also not counted (AtTartil's summary): «an authority God has not sent down» (8 times, e.g. al-A'raf 7:71); demands for, or mentions of, a miraculous sign being sent down (7 times, e.g. al-An'am 6:37); the devils descending (al-Shu'ara' 26:210, 26:221, 26:222); the false claim «I will send down» (al-An'am 6:93); and places read in more than one sense and not yet checked (apart from al-Talaq 65:10, which is counted; see below): 2:90 «an yunazzila Allahu min fadlihi» (al-Raghib lists it with favours), 2:102, 2:105, 8:41, 15:90, 17:102, 19:64, 53:13 and 65:12. Al-Talaq 65:10 «qad anzala Allahu ilaykum dhikran» is counted. Al-Raghib reports two views on it with «it was said» (qila): the sending down of dhikr (remembrance) there means the sending of the Prophet, or it means sending down mention of him. This root is not used by any other family.

Al-Raghib membezakan inzal (menurunkan) dengan tanzil (menurunkan beransur-ansur) apabila kedua-duanya dipakai untuk al-Quran dan malaikat. Menurut beliau, tanzil khusus untuk tempat yang menunjukkan bahawa ia diturunkan secara berasingan, sekali demi sekali. Inzal pula umum. Beliau menjelaskan bahawa dalam ad-Dukhan 44:3, al-Baqarah 2:185 dan al-Qadr 97:1 lafaz inzal yang dipilih, bukan tanzil, kerana ada riwayat yang menyebut al-Quran turun sekali gus ke langit dunia, kemudian turun sedikit demi sedikit.Al-Raghib distinguishes inzal (sending down) from tanzil (sending down in stages) when both describe the Qur'an and the angels. For him, tanzil is specific to places that indicate it was sent down in parts, time after time; inzal is general. He explains that in al-Dukhan 44:3, al-Baqara 2:185 and al-Qadr 97:1 the word inzal, not tanzil, is chosen because of a report that the Qur'an came down all at once to the lowest heaven, then came down little by little.

Petikan ini daripada al-Raghib, al-Mufradat fi Gharib al-Qur'an, fasal نزل (hlm. 799–800). Kitab al-Furuq al-Lughawiyyah oleh Abu Hilal al-'Askari tidak mempunyai fasal tentang beza kitab dengan wahy (wahyu), atau inzal (menurunkan) dengan tanzil (menurunkan beransur-ansur). Mu'jam al-Furuq al-Lughawiyyah ada fasal tentang beza ilham (bisikan ke dalam hati) dengan wahy, tetapi fasal itu tiada dalam al-Furuq al-Lughawiyyah, iaitu tambahan penyusun kemudian dan bukan kata al-'Askari, maka ia tidak dipetik.These quotes are from al-Raghib, al-Mufradat fi Gharib al-Qur'an, entry نزل (pp. 799–800). Abu Hilal al-'Askari's al-Furuq al-Lughawiyya has no entry on kitab versus wahy (revelation), or inzal (sending down) versus tanzil (sending down in stages). Mu'jam al-Furuq al-Lughawiyya has an entry on ilham (inspiration) versus wahy, but it is absent from al-Furuq al-Lughawiyya, i.e. a later compiler's addition and not al-'Askari's words, so it is not quoted.
