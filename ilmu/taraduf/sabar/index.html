SabarPatience

Keluarga makna · 2 kalimah al-QuranMeaning family · 2 Qur’anic words

Dalam bahasa Melayu, «sabar» dipakai untuk menahan diri dalam kesusahan dan juga untuk tidak cepat marah. Al-Quran memakai dua kalimah untuk dua makna ini. Al-Raghib al-Asfahani mentakrifkan sabr (menahan diri) sebagai menahan diri mengikut tuntutan akal dan syarak. Beliau mentakrifkan hilm (menahan marah dan tidak segera menghukum) sebagai mengawal diri daripada gelora marah. Abu Hilal al-'Askari menambah bahawa Allah disifatkan dengan hilm, tetapi tidak dengan sabr.In Malay, «sabar» covers both holding oneself firm in hardship and not being quick to anger. The Qur'an uses two words for these two senses. Al-Raghib al-Isfahani defines sabr (self-restraint) as restraining oneself as reason and the Sacred Law require. He defines hilm (restraining anger and not hastening punishment) as controlling oneself against the surge of anger. Abu Hilal al-'Askari adds that God is described with hilm, but not with sabr.

Semua bentuk akar ini dikira. Al-Raghib menukil Abu 'Ubaydah bahawa «فَمَآ أَصْبَرَهُمْ» dalam al-Baqarah 2:175 ialah satu loghat yang bermakna 'betapa beraninya mereka'. Al-Raghib sendiri menjelaskannya sebagai kiasan: betapa sabarnya mereka menanggung azab Allah, menurut anggapan mereka ketika berani melakukan dosa itu.All forms of the root are counted. Al-Raghib reports Abu 'Ubayda's view that «فَمَآ أَصْبَرَهُمْ» in al-Baqarah 2:175 is a dialect usage meaning 'how bold they are'. Al-Raghib himself explains it as a figure of speech: how patient they would be in bearing God's punishment, as they reckon it when they dare to commit that sin.

He says sabr is a general word. Among the verses he cites is al-Hajj 22:35, about those who are patient over what befalls them.

Hanya bentuk halim (yang sangat menahan marah) dikira; ia dipakai sebagai sifat Allah dan sifat beberapa nabi. Bentuk hulum (usia baligh) tidak dikira kerana maknanya lain.Only the form halim (very forbearing) is counted; it is used as an attribute of God and of some prophets. The form hulum (age of puberty) is not counted, as its sense differs.

Abu Hilal al-'Askari membezakan kedua-duanya. Pada beliau, hilm (menahan marah dan tidak segera menghukum) ialah memberi tangguh dengan melewatkan hukuman yang layak. Hilm hanya sah daripada orang yang mampu menghukum. Sabr (menahan diri) pula ialah menahan diri ketika bertemu perkara yang dibenci. Kerana itu, kata beliau, Allah tidak disifatkan dengan sabr, kerana kemudaratan tidak menimpa-Nya. Allah disifatkan dengan hilm, kerana ia pujian dan pengagungan.Abu Hilal al-'Askari distinguishes the two. For him, hilm (restraining anger and not hastening punishment) is granting respite by delaying a deserved punishment. Hilm is only valid from one who is able to punish. Sabr (self-restraint) is holding oneself back on meeting what is disliked. Hence, he says, God is not described with sabr, since harm does not reach Him. God is described with hilm, because it is praise and glorification.
