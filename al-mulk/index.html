Terjemahan: Katakanlah lagi: "Bagaimana fikiran kamu, sekiranya air kamu hilang lenyap (di telan bumi), maka siapakah (selain Allah) yang dapat mendatangkan kepada kamu air yang sentiasa terpancar mengalirTranslation: Say also, “What do you think: if your water were to vanish (swallowed into the earth), then who (other than Allah) could bring you water that springs up and flows without end?”

«وَالْمَقْصُودُ أَنْ يَجْعَلَهُمْ مُقِرِّينَ بِبَعْضِ نِعَمِهِ لِيُرِيَهُمْ قُبْحَ مَا هُمْ عَلَيْهِ مِنَ الْكُفْرِ»

Fakhr al-Dīn ar-Rāzī, Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb, 30/597Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī, Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb, 30/597

Kata ar-Rāzī, tujuan soalan ini ialah supaya mereka mengakui salah satu nikmat Allah, lalu nampak buruknya kekufuran mereka.
Renungan: Setiap kali minum air, mari kita sedar ia kurnia Allah, dan ucapkan alhamdulillah dengan hati.Al-Rāzī says the aim of this question is to make them acknowledge one of Allah’s blessings, and so see the ugliness of their disbelief.
Reflection: Whenever we drink water, let us recognise it as Allah’s gift and say “alhamdulillah” from the heart.
