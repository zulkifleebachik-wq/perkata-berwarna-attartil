Ciri nota: setiap kalimah diwarnakan mengikut jenisnya; makna perkata BM/EN; akar kata; dan kurungan fāʿil (pelaku) bagi setiap fiʿil — NF = nāʾib fāʿil (pengganti pelaku), ism = ism kāna (subjek kata kerja nāqiṣ). Fāʿil dirujuk kepada Iʿrāb al-Qur’ān al-Karīm, Dār al-Ṣaḥābah (Maktabah Shamela) dan disemak ATV (Arabic Turath Verifier). Terjemahan ayat: Tafsir Pimpinan ar-Rahman (Basmeih) · English: terjemahan AtTartil mengikut Basmeih.Features: every word coloured by type; BM/EN word meanings; root letters; and a fāʿil (doer) bracket for every verb — NF = nāʾib fāʿil (deputy doer), ism = subject of kāna. Fāʿil per Iʿrāb al-Qur’ān al-Karīm, Dār al-Ṣaḥābah (Maktabah Shamela), checked by ATV (Arabic Turath Verifier). Verse translations: Basmeih (Malay) · English: AtTartil translation following Basmeih.
