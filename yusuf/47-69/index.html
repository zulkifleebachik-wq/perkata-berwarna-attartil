«وَقَدْ مَزَجَ تَعْبِيرَهُ بِإِرْشَادٍ جَلِيلٍ لِأَحْوَالِ التَّمْوِينِ وَالِادِّخَارِ لِمَصْلَحَةِ الْأُمَّةِ»

Ibn ‘Āshūr, al-Taḥrīr wa al-Tanwīr, 12/286 (huraian ayat 47–49)Ibn ‘Āshūr, al-Taḥrīr wa al-Tanwīr, 12/286 (commentary on 47–49)

Menurut Ibn ‘Āshūr, Nabi Yusuf mentakbirkan mimpi raja itu meliputi semua yang ditunjukkannya. Dalam “Hendaklah kamu menanam bersungguh-sungguh tujuh tahun berturut-turut”, bertanam disebut kerana ia memang kebiasaan mereka; kata da’aban (berturut-turut) bermaksud kebiasaan dan berterusan melakukannya. Baginda menggabungkan takbir mimpi itu dengan bimbingan yang besar tentang pengurusan bekalan makanan dan simpanan demi kemaslahatan (kebaikan bersama) umat.
Renungan: Ilmu yang bermanfaat bukan sekadar menerangkan masalah, tetapi juga menunjukkan jalan keluar; marilah kita merancang lebih awal demi kebaikan keluarga dan masyarakat.According to Ibn ‘Āshūr, the Prophet Yusuf interpreted the king’s dream in full, covering all that it pointed to. Sowing is mentioned because farming was already their custom; da’aban, ‘one after another’, means a habit and keeping steadily to it. He blended his interpretation with weighty guidance on managing food supplies and stores for the common good of the community.
Reflection: Useful knowledge does not only explain a problem but also shows the way out; let us plan early for the good of our families and society.

«وَهَذَا تَحْرِيضٌ عَلَى اسْتِكْثَارِ الِادِّخَارِ»

Ibn ‘Āshūr, al-Taḥrīr wa al-Tanwīr, 12/287 (huraian ayat 47–49)Ibn ‘Āshūr, al-Taḥrīr wa al-Tanwīr, 12/287 (commentary on 47–49)

Menurut Ibn ‘Āshūr, Nabi Yusuf menasihati mereka supaya mengurangkan makan pada tahun-tahun subur dan menyimpan lebihannya untuk masa susah, serta membiarkannya pada tangkainya supaya lebih selamat daripada serangan kutu bijirin. Tahun-tahun susah itu dikatakan “menghabiskan makanan yang kamu sediakan baginya” secara kiasan, kerana pada masa itulah simpanan habis digunakan, kecuali sedikit yang tinggal di gudang. Kata beliau, ini dorongan supaya memperbanyak simpanan.
Renungan: Masa senang ialah peluang untuk bersedia menghadapi masa susah; marilah kita berjimat dan menyimpan sebahagian rezeki, tidak menghabiskan semuanya hari ini.According to Ibn ‘Āshūr, the Prophet Yusuf advised them to eat less in the fertile years and keep the surplus for hard times, leaving it in the ear where it is safer from grain weevils. The hard years are said to consume what was laid up for them figuratively, since that is when the stores are used up, save a little left in the granaries. This, he says, urges them to store in plenty.
Reflection: Times of ease are a chance to prepare for hardship; let us be thrifty and put part of our provision aside, rather than spending it all today.

«وَهَذَا يَدُلُّ عَلَى ذَهَابِ الْجَدْبِ وَحُصُولِ الْخِصْبِ وَالْخَيْرِ»

Ar-Rāzī menukil bahawa yughāth (beroleh rahmat hujan) bermaksud manusia diturunkan hujan; kata beliau, ia juga boleh bermaksud mereka diselamatkan daripada kesempitan musim kering. Maksud “padanya mereka dapat memerah” pula ialah mereka memerah hasil tanaman seperti bijan dan zaitun untuk diambil minyaknya, serta anggur. Kata beliau, ini menunjukkan musim kering telah berlalu dan kesuburan serta kebaikan telah tiba.
Renungan: Selepas kesempitan, Allah membuka pintu kelapangan; marilah kita tidak berputus asa ketika diuji, dan terus mengharapkan rahmat Allah.Ar-Rāzī reports that yughāth, receiving the mercy of rain, means people are given rain; he adds that it may also mean they are rescued from the distress of the dry years. Their pressing means pressing crops such as sesame and olives for their oil, and grapes too. This, he says, shows that the dry years have passed and that fertility and good have come.
Reflection: After distress Allah opens the door of relief; let us not lose hope when we are tested, and keep hoping for Allah’s mercy.

Pada pandangan ḥarf, لَمَّا akan diwarnakan merah di semua tempat itu. Kami mengikut kitab rujukan kami; maknanya tetap sama.Grammar note: فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُ ٱلرَّسُولُ (verse 50). لَمَّا is coloured green as an adverbial noun of time (ẓarf, “when”, Basmeih “tatkala”). Our reference book, Dār al-Ṣaḥābah (I‘rāb al-Qur’ān al-Karīm), says: “(لما): ظرف بمعنى حين تضمن معنى الشرط خافض لشرطه منصوب بجوابه” (lammā is a ẓarf meaning “when”, carrying the sense of a condition; its conditional clause is in the genitive and it is itself in the accusative by its answer) (2/1078); so too Maḥmūd Ṣāfī (al-Jadwal fī I‘rāb al-Qur’ān): “(لمّا) ظرف بمعنى حين متضمّن معنى الشرط” (6/447). Muḥyī al-Dīn Darwīsh (I‘rāb al-Qur’ān wa Bayānuh), however, says: “ولما حينية ظرفية أو رابطة” (lammā is a temporal ẓarf (“when”) or a linking particle (rābiṭah) that ties the condition to its answer) (5/6); and Muḥammad ‘Alī Ṭāhā al-Durrah (Tafsīr al-Qur’ān al-Karīm wa I‘rābuhu wa Bayānuh), in its i‘rāb (grammatical analysis) of verse 59, to which it refers for لَمَّا in verses 50, 54, 59, 63, 65, 66, 68 and 69, says: “(لما): حرف وجود لوجود عند سيبويه” (lammā is a particle “of existence because of existence” according to Sībawayh), and after giving the ẓarf view: “وصوب ابن هشام الأول، والمشهور الثاني” (Ibn Hishām judged the first view correct, and the better-known view is the second, the ẓarf) (4/616). On the particle view لَمَّا would be coloured red in all those places. We follow our reference book; the meaning stays the same.
✦ Ringkasan Tafsir & TadabburTafsir & Tadabbur Summary

«فَإِنَّهُ سُبْحَانَهُ جَعَلَ عِلْمَهُ سَبَبًا لِخَلَاصِهِ مِنَ الْمِحْنَةِ الدُّنْيَوِيَّةِ، فَكَيْفَ لَا يَكُونُ الْعِلْمُ سَبَبًا لِلْخَلَاصِ مِنَ الْمِحَنِ الْأُخْرَوِيَّةِ»

Fakhr al-Dīn ar-Rāzī, Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb, 18/466 (huraian ayat 50–52)Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī, Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb, 18/466 (commentary on 50–52)

Kata ar-Rāzī, perintah raja “Bawalah dia kepadaku!” setelah mendengar takbir itu menunjukkan kelebihan ilmu: Allah menjadikan ilmu Nabi Yusuf sebab baginda terlepas daripada ujian dunia, maka lebih-lebih lagi ilmu menjadi sebab terselamat daripada ujian akhirat. Namun baginda enggan keluar dari penjara sehingga perkaranya terbongkar dan tuduhan itu hilang sepenuhnya. Menurut beliau, kesabaran itulah yang sesuai dengan kebijaksanaan; antara alasan yang beliau sebut, jika baginda terus keluar, mungkin masih tinggal kesan tuduhan itu dalam hati raja.
Renungan: Ilmu menyelamatkan kita di dunia dan akhirat, dan nama yang bersih lebih berharga daripada kebebasan yang tergesa-gesa; marilah kita menuntut ilmu dan menjaga maruah dengan sabar.Ar-Rāzī says that the king’s order to bring him, given after hearing the interpretation, shows the virtue of knowledge: Allah made the Prophet Yusuf’s knowledge the cause of his release from a worldly trial, so all the more can knowledge be a cause of rescue from the trials of the Hereafter. Yet he refused to leave prison until his case was laid open and the charge wholly removed. In his view that patience was what prudence called for; among the reasons he gives, had he left at once, some trace of the charge might have stayed in the king’s heart.
Reflection: Knowledge saves us in this world and the next, and a clean name is worth more than hasty freedom; let us seek knowledge and guard our honour with patience.

Reflection: We ought never to think ourselves pure; let us keep asking for Allah’s mercy to be kept far from the pull of the self.

Our reference book, Dār al-Ṣaḥābah (I‘rāb al-Qur’ān al-Karīm), says: “وجملة: كان ومتعلقاتها صلة الموصول” (the clause of kāna with what belongs to it is the ṣilah (completing clause) of the relative noun) (2/1088); so too Maḥmūd Ṣāfī (al-Jadwal fī I‘rāb al-Qur’ān): “(ما) اسم موصول مبني في محلّ جرّ متعلّق ب (تبتئس)” (7/30), and Muḥyī al-Dīn Darwīsh (I‘rāb al-Qur’ān wa Bayānuh): “وبما متعلقان بتبتئس وجملة كانوا صلة” (5/25). Muḥammad ‘Alī Ṭāhā al-Durrah (Tafsīr al-Qur’ān al-Karīm wa I‘rābuhu wa Bayānuh), however, says: “و (ما) تحتمل الموصولة، والموصوفة، والمصدرية” (mā may be a relative noun, a described noun, or maṣdariyyah) (4/628). On the maṣdariyyah view مَا is a particle and would be coloured red, meaning “because of their doing”. We follow our reference book; the word meanings follow Basmeih.
(2) Here لَمَّا (“when”) is coloured green as a ẓarf, as in verse 50; the particle (ḥarf) view and its colour are given in the note on verse 50.

«فَأَرَادَ أَنْ يَجْعَلَ قَلْبَ أَخِيهِ صَافِيًا معهم أَيْضًا»

Fakhr al-Dīn ar-Rāzī, Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb, 18/485 (huraian ayat 69–72)Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī, Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb, 18/485 (commentary on 69–72)

Antara huraian ar-Rāzī tentang “janganlah engkau berdukacita lagi disebabkan apa yang mereka lakukan”: Nabi Yusuf tidak lagi menyimpan sebarang permusuhan dalam hatinya terhadap saudara-saudaranya, lalu baginda mahu hati saudaranya itu juga bersih terhadap mereka, iaitu tidak lagi menghiraukan apa yang mereka lakukan dahulu. Kata beliau, pendapat yang sahih, iaitu pendapat para mufassir, ialah baginda memperkenalkan dirinya sebagai saudara senasab, kerana itu lebih kuat menghilangkan rasa sunyi dan mendatangkan kemesraan.
Renungan: Hati yang bersih daripada dendam mampu merapatkan keluarga; marilah kita memaafkan, dan membantu orang lain melepaskan luka lama.Among ar-Rāzī’s explanations of telling his brother not to grieve over what they had done: the Prophet Yusuf no longer held any enmity in his heart towards his brothers, and he wished his brother’s heart to be clear towards them as well, paying no more heed to what they had done before. He says the sound view, held by the exegetes, is that he made himself known as his brother by blood, for that does more to remove loneliness and bring comfort.
Reflection: A heart free of grudges can draw a family together; let us forgive, and help others let go of old wounds.
